# StartPos Studio

Everything about StartPos in one place, made to be simple.

## Before you play
Press the green flag button on the level page. You get a **map of the level** with every StartPos as a flag.
Tap a flag and press **Play**: the level starts there.

## Parts with a start and an end
Every StartPos is the start of a part. The part ends at the next StartPos, or where you choose with **Set end** on the map.
When you reach the end you get **SECTION COMPLETE!** and the part is marked as done (you see "done x3" on the map),
but the level keeps going so you can keep practising.

## While you play
At the top left you see:
- your **%** and the **goal** of your part,
- **CPS**: clicks per second,
- **Clicks**: clicks in this try (back to 0 when you die),
- how many times you completed this part.

Keys:
- **Q / E**: previous / next StartPos.
- **' (quote key)** in practice mode: puts a StartPos where you are, with the right gamemode, speed, size, gravity and dual.
- **Shift + '**: deletes the StartPos you placed.
- **Auto-fix**: when you fly through a StartPos, the mod checks it. If it has the wrong gamemode or speed, it fixes it (only for you).

## Your best try
The mod records your tries, like a bot: your path and every jump.
- The map shows **only your best try** as one line (colour = gamemode), each jump as a white dot.
- **Watch** plays your best try on the map: your icon goes through the level and the map follows it.
- **Tap your line** (or a jump) and press **Place here**: the StartPos gets the gamemode, speed, size and gravity you had there.
- Deaths are **optional** and off by default: the **Deaths** button shows or hides the red X, on the map and in the level.

## Share
**Copy code** puts your StartPos in a code (starts with `SPS1:`). Post it in the level comments or on Discord.
Anyone with the mod copies it and presses **Paste** in the map.

All the keys can be changed in the mod settings.
