# StartPos Studio

Everything about StartPos in one place, made to be simple.

## The map
Press the green flag button on the level page (or in the pause menu). You get a **map of the level**:
- every StartPos is a **flag** with its number or its name,
- your **best try** is a line (colour = gamemode) with the **%** along it and a white dot for every jump.

Tap a flag and press **Play**: the level starts there. **Rename** gives it a name, **Delete** removes it
(StartPos of the level are only hidden for you: Share > Bring back).
Tap your line and press **Add StartPos here**: it gets the gamemode, speed, size and gravity you had there.

## Routes
A route is a part you want to practise, like **50% to 100%** one day and **60% to 75%** another day.
Choose a flag, press **Routes > New route** and tap where it ends. Then press **Play**.
When you reach the end of the route you go straight back to its StartPos, and it counts as done.
In **Routes** you can play, rename and delete them.

## While you play
At the top left (each one can be turned off in the settings):
- your **%** (and the end of your route),
- **CPS**: clicks per second,
- **Clicks**: clicks in this try (back to 0 when you die).

Keys:
- **Q / E**: previous / next StartPos.
- **' (quote key)** in practice mode: puts a StartPos where you are, with the right gamemode, speed, size, gravity and dual.
- **Shift + '**: deletes the StartPos you placed.
- **Auto-fix**: when you fly through a StartPos, the mod checks it and fixes a wrong gamemode or speed (only for you).

## More
- **Watch** plays your best try on the map.
- **Deaths** shows or hides red X where you died (on the map and in the level). Off by default.
- **Share**: your StartPos in one code (starts with `SPS1:`) to post in the comments or on Discord, and **Paste code** to add someone else's.
