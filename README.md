# StartPos Studio

A Geode mod for Geometry Dash 2.2081 (Windows) that puts everything about StartPos in one place.

- **Map of the level** before you play: every StartPos is a flag, tap one and press Play to start there.
- **Switcher** while playing: Q / E for previous / next StartPos.
- **Place a StartPos with one key** (`'`) in practice mode, with the right gamemode, speed, size, gravity and dual.
- **Auto-fix**: StartPos with a wrong gamemode or speed get corrected when you fly through them.
- **Your runs**: your path, every jump and where you died, shown on the map and in the level (plus a ghost of your best run). Tap your line on the map to put a StartPos there.
- **Share codes** (`SPS1:...`) to pass your StartPos to other players.

## Install
1. Download `traxcerd.startpos-studio.geode` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Put it in `Geometry Dash/geode/mods`.
3. Restart the game.

If you use another StartPos switcher, turn it off (same keys).
